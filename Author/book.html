Currently serializing — working title

Khoon Aur Ishq

"Blood and Love." A sixty-chapter mafia romance between two Pakistani-American families bound by a debt older than either of the two people now expected to pay it. Also drafted under the working title Zakhm-e-Ishq ("Wound of Love").

Synopsis

A marriage neither of them chose.

Twenty years ago, a favor changed hands between two families — the kind of favor that isn't repaid in money. When the debt finally comes due, it's collected in the form of a marriage: her, the daughter raised to read every room she walks into; him, the son raised to never need to. Neither agreed to this. Both are too proud to refuse it out loud.

What begins as two families performing peace for each other becomes something neither the debt nor the marriage accounted for — and the closer they get, the more dangerous it becomes for either of them to be caught wanting it.
