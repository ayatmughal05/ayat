About

The name behind the story.

Aythrix is the pen name under which I write — a space kept separate from my other work so the fiction can stay exactly as dramatic, messy, and unresolved as it needs to be.

Why Aythrix

A name that belongs only to the page.

I write Urdu-inflected, Pakistani-American romance — stories that move between two languages and two ideas of home, often within the same sentence. The pen name isn't a disguise so much as a boundary: it lets the writing be as unfiltered as it needs to be, without asking my everyday name to carry that weight.

Currently serializing Khoon Aur Ishq, a sixty-chapter mafia romance built around a debt, a marriage, and two families who mistake control for safety.

Timeline

How this started

  • Origin

    First drafts, privately

    Started writing Urdu-inflected romance for myself before ever posting it anywhere — working out what a diaspora love story sounds like when it isn't translated for anyone.

  • Early serials

    First posted chapters

    Began serializing shorter romance stories across Wattpad and Inkitt to learn pacing in public, chapter by chapter, with real reader feedback.

  • Current

    Khoon Aur Ishq

    Now developing a full sixty-chapter mafia romance — the most structurally ambitious project under the Aythrix name so far, mapped chapter by chapter before drafting began.
