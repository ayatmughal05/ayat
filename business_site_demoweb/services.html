Services — Sheet A‑002

Four ways to work with us.

Most clients fall into one of four categories below. If yours doesn't, get in touch anyway — we'll tell you honestly whether we're the right fit.

01

Residential architecture

New homes and full renovations, led start to finish by one principal.
  • Site analysis & feasibility
  • Concept & schematic design
  • Construction documents & permitting
  • Construction administration
02

Commercial fit‑out

Cafés, studios, and small retail spaces where layout and material choices need to earn their keep.
  • Space planning & code review
  • Millwork & fixture design
  • Lighting & material specification
  • Contractor bidding support
03

Interiors

Standalone interiors work for clients who already have architectural drawings and need the inside finished.
  • Material & finish selection
  • Custom furniture & joinery
  • Lighting design
  • Procurement coordination
04

Design consulting

A single working session — two to three hours — for clients with a builder already in place who need a second opinion on plan or material decisions.
  • Plan & massing review
  • Material direction
  • Written summary of recommendations
