About — Sheet A‑001

Four people, one drafting table.

Isole Studio was founded in 2016 on a simple premise: a house should be designed around how you actually move through your day, not around a style borrowed from a magazine.

Philosophy

We design the ordinary moments first.

Most of what happens in a home is unremarkable — making coffee, finding a coat, catching the morning light while you brush your teeth. We start every project by mapping those moments, then let the plan, the materials, and the massing follow from them. The dramatic room comes later, and usually costs less than clients expect once the ordinary rooms are right.

That approach means slower first meetings and a longer design phase than some studios prefer. It also means fewer change orders once construction starts, because the plan was tested against real routines before it was tested against a budget.

How a project runs

Five stages, roughly in order

Every project moves through these stages — the sequence matters, so we number it.

  1. Discovery

    Site visit, budget conversation, and a walk‑through of how you actually use your current space. Two to three weeks.

  2. Concept

    Rough massing, plan diagrams, and a materials direction. This is where the project's few big decisions get made.

  3. Design development

    Fixed plans, elevations, and material specifications — enough detail to price the project accurately.

  4. Construction documents

    Permit‑ready drawing set, structural coordination, and contractor bidding.

  5. Construction administration

    Site visits through build‑out to confirm the drawings are being read correctly on site.
